# Acme Corp Employee Benefits Guide

## Health Insurance
Acme covers 90% of the monthly premium for employees and 70% for dependents.
Coverage starts on the first day of the month following the hire date.

## Paid Time Off
Full-time employees receive 25 days of paid vacation per year, plus 10 public holidays.
Unused vacation days up to 5 may be carried over into the next year.

## Parental Leave
Primary caregivers receive 16 weeks of fully paid parental leave. Secondary caregivers receive 6 weeks.

## Learning Budget
Each employee has an annual learning budget of $1,200 for courses, books and conferences.
Manager approval is required for any single expense above $500.

## Home Office
Employees working remotely receive a home office stipend and an internet allowance
as described in the Remote Work Policy.
