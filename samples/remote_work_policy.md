# Acme Corp Remote Work Policy

## Eligibility
All full-time employees who have completed their 90-day probation period may request remote work.
Contractors and interns are not eligible for remote work arrangements.

## Working Hours
Remote employees must be reachable between 10:00 and 16:00 in their team's primary time zone.
Core meetings are scheduled inside this window. Outside it, employees may work flexible hours.

## Equipment and Expenses
Acme provides a laptop and a monitor. Employees receive a one-time home office stipend of $500
and a monthly internet allowance of $40. Receipts must be submitted within 30 days.

## Security
Remote employees must use the company VPN at all times when accessing internal systems.
Personal devices may not be used to store customer data. Lost devices must be reported
to the security team within 2 hours.

## In-Person Days
Teams must meet in the office at least 2 days per quarter for planning sessions.
Travel costs for mandatory in-person days are reimbursed in full.
